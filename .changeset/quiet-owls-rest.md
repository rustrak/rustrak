Auto resolve: a project setting (1 to 720 hours) that resolves unresolved issues once they go that long without an event, checked every 10 minutes and mirrored to GitHub. Logs keep their environment, with an Environment filter and a table whose lines open in place. The server gives memory back after ingest bursts: variable-size inserts are no longer cached, the SQLite pool defaults to 4 connections, and password hashing runs one at a time. Project cards keep their figures inside the card.
